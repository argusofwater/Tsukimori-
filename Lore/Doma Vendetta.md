# Doma Vendetta

## Personal stake

Doma is explicitly established as the demon who killed [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu|Kageharu's]] family.

That makes Doma one of Kageharu's two primary personal vendettas alongside Kokushibo.

## Intelligence from Daki

During [[Arcs/Entertainment District AU]], Daki revealed:

- Doma found dying Daki and Gyutaro and offered them blood.
- Doma is connected to the `Eternal Paradise Faith`.
- Followers come to him willingly to worship and confess.
- Women are especially relevant to his interests.
- Doma presents as beautiful, smiling, kind, and joking, but this presentation is false.
- Daki warned Kageharu: `don't let him talk.`
- Daki did not know or did not provide Doma's exact current location.

## Investigation leads

Potential ways to find him:

- cult recruiters
- traveling followers
- donation trails
- disappearances associated with worshippers
- social networks around the Eternal Paradise Faith

## Continuity lock

The group currently has intelligence, not a location.

Do not teleport the story directly to Doma without developing the trail unless the user explicitly chooses to.
