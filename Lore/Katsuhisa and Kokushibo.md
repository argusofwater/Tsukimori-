# Katsuhisa and Kokushibo

## Michikatsu Tsugikuni

Human name of the swordsman who later became the demon Kokushibo.

Established in this AU:

- creator/user of Moon Breathing
- older twin brother of Yoriichi Tsugikuni
- father of [[Characters/Katsuhisa Tsugikuni]]
- willingly became a demon
- later forced Katsuhisa into demonhood against Katsuhisa's will

## Katsuhisa

Katsuhisa is Michikatsu's direct son, born while Michikatsu was still human and only 19 years younger than him.

Katsuhisa rejected his father's choices and eventually developed [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]] as a purpose-built answer to Moon Breathing.

## Kageharu's connection

Katsuhisa found [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu]] at age 12 and trained/raised him for seven years.

Kageharu and Katsuhisa later fought Kokushibo together for at least two hours.

Important established beats:

- rotating strike / retreat / recovery tactics
- Kageharu attempted Seventh Form
- Kokushibo fired a blade from a finger
- that attack left the scar across Kageharu's chest
- Kageharu was later nearly cut in half
- seeing him fall broke Katsuhisa's concentration
- Katsuhisa showed the deeper full expression of Veil Breathing
- Kokushibo killed Katsuhisa
- Kageharu survived because Tamayo later saved him

## Current vendetta

Kageharu wants Kokushibo dead for Katsuhisa's death.

He has already told Tengen's group about this history during the Entertainment District mission.

## Continuity caution

Do not assume Kageharu knows every detail of Kokushibo's canon history. What he knows should come from Katsuhisa, direct encounters, Kagaya, or later story discovery.
