## Purpose

The goal is simple: keep this increasingly enormous story from eating its own tail.

Facts are separated into three categories:

- **LOCKED**: explicitly established in roleplay and should not be contradicted.
- **OPEN**: established question or unresolved thread.
- **INFERENCE**: reasonable interpretation, but not confirmed canon unless later established.

When in doubt, [[Continuity/Canon Locks]] outranks comedy, assumptions, and memory drift.
