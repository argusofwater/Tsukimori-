# Moonveil

Kageharu's most dangerous sword and the actual sword of [[Characters/Katsuhisa Tsugikuni]].

## Appearance

- Strange silver blade.
- Black sheath.
- Pommel/tip motif resembles a rounded flower bloom opening into a moon.
- Handle is unusually long, roughly three fists.
- Blade is roughly four handle-lengths.

## Function

Moonveil is the blade around which the full expression of [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]] was built.

Kageharu's usual sword cannot safely channel the same maximum output. Moonveil can.

This makes Moonveil both stronger and more dangerous to Kageharu. Katsuhisa's demon body could survive forces and recoil that a human body may not tolerate.

Even a small technical mistake in Silent Crescent while using Moonveil could hurt Kageharu badly.

## Seventh Form

Moonveil was used for `Hi no Kata: Mangetsu no Kanata....` during the Entertainment District battle.

The technique's extreme environmental effect and aftermath are detailed in [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]].
