# Relationship Map

### Mentor / family

- [[Characters/Katsuhisa Tsugikuni]]: deceased mentor, guardian, and effective family.

- Mitsuri Kanroji: warm, easy rapport, frequent comedy.
- Obanai Iguro: cautious but increasingly familiar.
- Giyu Tomioka: quiet coexistence.
- Gyomei Himejima: serious respect.
- Muichiro Tokito: sword-focused curiosity.
- Kyojuro Rengoku: alive in this AU and interested in future training/sparring.
- Kagaya Ubuyashiki: accepted Kageharu into the Corps and allowed his Kinoe-by-request arrangement.

## Important emotional lines

### Kageharu ↔ Shinobu

`my moon` / `my butterfly`

Core tension: love and future-building against Kageharu's expectation that good things can disappear.

### Kageharu ↔ Tengen

Core tension: competent professionals who respect one another while behaving like brothers trying to see who can make the other regret speaking first.

### Kageharu ↔ Tanjiro

Core tension: older-brother instruction without trying to replace Tanjiro's own path.

### Kageharu ↔ Inosuke

Core tension: channeling Inosuke's instincts rather than suppressing them.

### Kageharu ↔ Zenitsu

Core tension: teasing him mercilessly while quietly taking his potential seriously.
