# Shinobu and Kageharu

## Current state

Active mutual courtship. Serious romantic intent. Not married. Not formally engaged.

## Dynamic

Kageharu is openly devoted and often theatrical through flowers, food, and practical care. Shinobu is more guarded, but the affection is explicit and reciprocated.

Their relationship works best when Shinobu remains clever, independent, medically competent, and fully capable of setting boundaries rather than becoming merely flustered by Kageharu.

Kageharu's deepest emotional problem is not whether Shinobu likes him. It is his expectation that good things can disappear because so much of his family and life already did.
