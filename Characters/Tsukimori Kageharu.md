# Tsukimori Kageharu

## Core identity

- Age: 19.
- Setting: Demon Slayer AU.
- Formal Corps rank: Kinoe by personal request until he officially earns anything higher.
- Actual combat level: roughly Hashira-comparable.
- Before joining the Corps he independently protected his home province and three villages around his mountain.
- He had personally killed 185 demons before the current Entertainment District mission.
- He does **not** consider himself a master and does not want students calling him one.

## Appearance and equipment

- Normally wears his own clothing rather than a standard Corps uniform.
- Signature long haori bears the kanji `月` for moon.
- Entertainment District mission outfit: black fitted shinobi wraps/layers, loose dark trousers, shortened layered outer fabric, long divided mantle with large `月`, lunar charms at the waist.
- Carries [[Equipment/Moonveil]] on his back.
- Also carries an odachi.
- His usual silver Nichirin sword is described in [[Equipment/Usual Silver Nichirin Sword]].

## Personality

Kageharu is calm, polite, dryly funny, and physically controlled to an eerie degree. He is also a committed troll when he decides someone deserves it. He tends to treat younger slayers as younger siblings rather than subordinates.

He is practical about food, rest, hydration, fieldcraft, and survival. He believes in expecting problems rather than disturbing every possible danger just to prove it exists.

A recurring personal philosophy is that the moon teaches through what is needed rather than what is wanted. His home villages reflect this in practical mutual support, food, firewood, wisteria, and shared preparedness.

## Family and loss

- Demons killed his parents and five younger siblings.
- [[Lore/Doma Vendetta|Doma]] is specifically established as the demon who killed his family.
- Kageharu buried them and wandered afterward.
- At age 12 he was found by [[Characters/Katsuhisa Tsugikuni]].
- Katsuhisa trained and raised him for seven years.
- Kageharu often frames the people around him as family because he lost his own so young.

## Skills outside combat

Katsuhisa deliberately prepared him to survive a life beyond swordsmanship.

Established skills include:

- cooking and meal preparation
- preserving food
- sewing and clothing repair
- forging
- botany
- grooming and eyebrow care
- oils, lotions, fragrances, and aromatherapy
- mechanotherapy/massage
- farming and household work
- flower symbolism and courtship etiquette
- perfume and grooming knowledge
- bamboo food packing

These later became the joking framework called [[Abilities/Domestic Breathing]].

## Relationships

### [[Characters/Shinobu Kocho]]

Active mutual courtship. They have kissed and discussed a possible future, home, clinic, children, and marriage. They are **not** married or formally engaged yet.

Kageharu calls her `Shinobu-chan`, `my butterfly`, and `favorite butterfly`. Shinobu has called him `my moon`.

See [[Relationships/Shinobu and Kageharu]].

### [[Characters/Tanjiro Kamado]]

Kageharu treats Tanjiro as a younger brother and considers him the best of that informal younger-brother group. He has taught him economy, quick-draw principles, and the philosophy behind Silent Crescent without teaching Veil Breathing itself.

### [[Characters/Zenitsu Agatsuma]]

Also treated as a younger brother, although Kageharu relentlessly teases him. Kageharu has discussed a personalized `Silent Lightning` concept for future training.

### [[Characters/Inosuke Hashibira]]

Younger-brother dynamic. Kageharu developed the `Awaiting Cobra` sensory discipline with him and has also, on more than one occasion, solved Inosuke's refusal to rest by hiding a fast-acting sleep medicine in food.

### [[Characters/Tengen Uzui]]

Mutual respect buried under constant verbal warfare. Tengen is the commanding Hashira for the Entertainment District mission and takes Kageharu's injuries seriously.

See [[Relationships/Kageharu and Tengen]].

## Current physical state

See [[Continuity/Current State]].

After using the secret Seventh Form of [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]] against Daki and Gyutaro, observed consequences included:

- blood from one eye
- severe exhaustion
- persistent right-hand tremor
- temporarily measured/abnormal gait
- later improvement in gait and tremor
- occasional involuntary twitching in the right arm the following morning
- Kageharu suspects a forearm muscle injury, but this is **not medically confirmed**
- Kageharu also reported that a muscle around the right diaphragm remained tense, again **not medically confirmed**

[[Characters/Shinobu Kocho]] must be allowed to evaluate rather than the story treating these self-diagnoses as fact.

## Important boundaries

- User controls Kageharu's choices, dialogue, and voluntary actions in roleplay.
- Do not auto-promote him to Hashira.
- Do not make him a literal Moon Hashira unless Kagaya formally grants that title in story.
- Do not simplify his Seventh Form into an ordinary sword technique.
- Do not make his relationship with Shinobu suddenly become marriage without an actual proposal and acceptance.
