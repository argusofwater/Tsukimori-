# Katsuhisa Tsugikuni

## Core identity

- Direct son of Michikatsu Tsugikuni, later known as [[Lore/Katsuhisa and Kokushibo|Kokushibo]].
- Born while Michikatsu was still human.
- Only 19 years younger than his father.
- Eventually made into a demon unwillingly by his father.
- Hated the choice forced on him and resisted the values of Kokushibo.
- Controlled his hunger through voluntarily donated blood.
- Created [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]] specifically as an answer to Moon Breathing and his father.
- Trained [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu]] for seven years, beginning when Kageharu was 12.

## Relationship to Kageharu

Katsuhisa was mentor, guardian, and effectively family.

He did not merely teach swordsmanship. He deliberately prepared Kageharu for ordinary life after violence, teaching cooking, preservation, sewing, forging, botany, grooming, household care, courtship traditions, and many other practical skills.

This broad education is the origin of the later joking framework [[Abilities/Domestic Breathing]].

## Final conflict

Katsuhisa and Kageharu fought Kokushibo together around 1.5 years before the current story.

Established elements:

- The fight lasted at least two hours.
- Katsuhisa and Kageharu rotated between striking, retreating, and recovering.
- Kageharu attempted the secret Seventh Form.
- It worked only partially and left him vulnerable.
- Kokushibo fired a blade attack from a finger and left the scar across Kageharu's chest.
- Kageharu was later struck down and nearly cut in half.
- Seeing Kageharu fall broke Katsuhisa's concentration.
- Katsuhisa then fully revealed the deeper extent of Veil Breathing.
- Kokushibo ultimately killed Katsuhisa.
- Kageharu survived only because [[Lore/Tamayo Connection|Tamayo]] later saved him.

## Legacy

Kageharu carries Katsuhisa's true sword, [[Equipment/Moonveil]].

Katsuhisa's core lesson is not only power. It is survival, restraint, adaptability, and having a life worth returning to after combat.
