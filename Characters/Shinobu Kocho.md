# Shinobu Kocho

## Role in this AU

Shinobu remains recognizably herself: observant, medically exacting, independent, polite even when angry, and often smiling through sharper emotions. She is not softened into a passive romantic interest.

She is [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu|Kageharu's]] active romantic partner in early courtship.

## Relationship state

- Mutual romantic interest is explicit.
- They have shared forehead, cheek, and lip kisses.
- A deeper passionate kiss occurred the morning Kageharu departed for the Entertainment District mission.
- They have discussed hypothetical marriage, children, a clinic, and a peaceful future after Muzan.
- They are **not married** and **not formally engaged**.
- Kageharu intends to make a proper proposal later.

See [[Relationships/Shinobu and Kageharu]].

She asked Aoi to prepare examination supplies and a reinforced examination table.
