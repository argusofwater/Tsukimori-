# Tengen Uzui

## AU status

- Alive.
- Both arms intact.
- Age 23.
- This story occurs before the canon Entertainment District arc.
- Husband of Suma, Makio, and Hinatsuru.

## Relationship with Kageharu

Tengen and [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu]] have a strong professional respect buried under constant competitive banter.

Tengen was operational commander for [[Arcs/Entertainment District AU]]. He explicitly told Kageharu to speak if he noticed something Tengen missed.

He takes Kageharu's injuries seriously and does not let comedy override command responsibility.

## Major running jokes

- Kageharu called him old during a fake argument.
- Tengen shouted `I AM TWENTY-THREE!`
- His wives later caught him making a joke about surviving twenty years of marriage.
- `Old Man Tengen` became a recurring target.
- Kageharu complimented Tengen's powerful thighs while disguised as a woman.
- Tengen smiled before realizing who it was.
- Kageharu later weaponized this knowledge repeatedly.
- After Kageharu sleep-talked `the jewels need more polish. Get flashier`, Tengen seriously considered whether his jewelry required more polishing.
