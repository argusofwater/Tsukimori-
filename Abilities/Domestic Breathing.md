# Domestic Breathing

A joking pseudo-Breathing Style created during breakfast at Tengen's house after the Entertainment District battle.

It is not a combat style. It is Kageharu's framework for being a competent and attentive partner, built from the domestic skills Katsuhisa taught him.

## Forms

### First Form: House Care

Basic household competence:

- dishes
- sweeping
- laundry
- cleaning
- general upkeep

### Second Form: Basic Cooking

Be capable of reliably making breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

### Third Form: Partner Awareness

Notice subtle changes in a partner's behavior and adapt support appropriately.

Core idea: sometimes people do not fully understand or articulate their own emotions yet, but a good partner still tries to be present without assuming too much.

### Fourth Form: Courtship

Flowers, poems, gestures, and whatever personal style the couple adds.

See [[Motifs/Flower Language and Courtship]].

### Fifth Form: Unprompted Care

Do useful or loving things for a partner without waiting to be asked.

### Sixth Form: Mechanotherapy and Aromatherapy

Massage, bodywork, oils, scents, lotions, and related comfort skills.

Kageharu demonstrated this on Makio, Suma, and Hinatsuru after receiving permission from each of them.

### Seventh Form: Homemade Dessert Pan

The breakfast joke escalated when Kageharu produced homemade dessert bread and called it the Seventh Form.

### Eighth Form

Exists, but Kageharu told the younger group they were not old enough for that lesson and to come back at eighteen.

The exact content was intentionally left unstated in scene.

### Ninth Form: Marriage

Tengen jokingly named `Marriage` the Ninth Form later in the same breakfast scene.

## Important continuity note

Domestic Breathing is a comedy framework, but the underlying skills are genuinely part of Kageharu's established upbringing and practical competence.
