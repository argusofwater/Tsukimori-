# Veil Breathing

## Origin

Created by [[Characters/Katsuhisa Tsugikuni]] specifically as an answer to his father Michikatsu Tsugikuni, later [[Lore/Katsuhisa and Kokushibo|Kokushibo]], and therefore as a counterstyle to Moon Breathing.

The style is not simply Moon Breathing renamed. Its central discipline is eliminating waste, disguising intent, controlling transitions, and turning saved motion/force into increasingly dangerous effects.

## Known Forms

1. **Silent Crescent**
2. **Azure Arc**
3. **Lunar Reflection**
4. **Veil of Waning Moon**
5. **Full Moon Severance**
6. **Pale Night, Endless Sky**
7. **Hi no Kata: Mangetsu no Kanata....**

The Seventh is secret and restricted.

## First Form: Silent Crescent

The philosophical and technical foundation of the style.

- Economy of motion.
- Remove tells.
- Remove wasted movement.
- The First Form is never truly considered fully mastered.
- Later forms continually test the First.

Kageharu has adapted its principles into other training systems without teaching Veil Breathing itself, including Inosuke's `Awaiting Cobra` and Zenitsu's proposed `Silent Lightning`.

## Second Form: Azure Arc

Saved energy is released into a visible cutting pressure wave with moonlight-like sparkle.

This effect is physically real in the AU, not merely visual metaphor.

### Perfected Second Form

During the Entertainment District battle, Kageharu used a perfected version that launched a ground-running moonlit pressure wave, cut through Daki's obi, struck her, and blasted her into a building. Her regeneration was not instantaneous.

## Third Form: Lunar Reflection

Counterdraw-oriented form.

## Fourth Form: Veil of Waning Moon

Five separate attacks executed so rapidly and efficiently they can appear simultaneous. Each attack remains individually meaningful and can be adaptively allocated.

## Fifth Form: Full Moon Severance

Heavy, concentrated killing slice.

## Sixth Form: Pale Night, Endless Sky

An evolved expression of the Fourth.

- Repeated blooming draws.
- Constant sequence where each movement blends into the next.
- No fixed attack count. The answer is simply `enough`.
- Extremely demanding on the body and concentration.
- Kageharu has combined Sixth with five Second-Form pressure waves, crossing a limit he had previously not reached.
- Katsuhisa was capable of this level.

If Kageharu loses track or develops tunnel vision, the Sixth should end. Core failure or compensatory movement are medical concerns.

## Seventh Form

### Exact name

`Hi no Kata: Mangetsu no Kanata....`

Do not rename or translate unless the user explicitly does so.

### Known battlefield effect

During the Entertainment District climax:

- Kageharu ordered allies to create distance and leave populated space.
- He vanished and reappeared behind Daki and Gyutaro.
- He slowly sheathed [[Equipment/Moonveil]].
- At full seating, thousands of moonlight slashes manifested throughout the surrounding area.
- The effect struck demons, trees, leaves, insects, earth, and the environment broadly.
- Daki's throat was deliberately excluded.
- Gyutaro was not spared.
- Both demons were reduced to mince-like states but regenerated because their simultaneous decapitation condition had not yet been fulfilled.

Tanjiro recognized that the control was expressed not only through where the cuts existed, but through where they did **not**.

### Danger

The Seventh is not to be taught.

Insufficient control could massacre everything around the user. Tengen agreed that its mechanics should not appear in the general Corps report.

### Observed cost to Kageharu

- blood from one eye
- severe depletion
- right-hand tremor
- abnormal measured gait
- later residual right-arm twitching
- possible additional internal/muscular consequences not yet diagnosed

Kageharu has also previously used the Seventh against Kokushibo and paid heavily for it.

## Moonveil warning

[[Equipment/Moonveil]] can take Veil Breathing at full strength. Kageharu's ordinary sword cannot safely express the same output.

A mistake in even the First Form while using Moonveil can hurt Kageharu drastically because Katsuhisa had a demon body capable of surviving forces/recoil a human body may not.

The exact mechanism is intentionally not fully defined yet.
