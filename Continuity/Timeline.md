# Timeline

## Childhood

- Kageharu is born.
- Demons eventually kill his parents and five younger siblings.
- Doma is established as the demon responsible for his family's deaths.
- Kageharu buries his family and wanders.

## Age 12

- Katsuhisa Tsugikuni finds Kageharu.
- Seven years of training and upbringing begin.

## Ages 12 to 19

Katsuhisa teaches:

- Veil Breathing
- survival
- cooking
- preserving food
- sewing
- forging
- botany
- household care
- grooming
- flower language and courtship
- practical life beyond combat

## Roughly 1.5 years before current mission

- Kageharu and Katsuhisa confront Kokushibo.
- Fight lasts at least two hours.
- Kageharu attempts the secret Seventh Form.
- Kokushibo wounds Kageharu badly, including the chest scar.
- Kageharu is later nearly cut in half.
- Katsuhisa's concentration breaks when Kageharu falls.
- Katsuhisa fully reveals deeper Veil capability.
- Kokushibo kills Katsuhisa.
- Kageharu is left for dead.
- Tamayo saves Kageharu.

## Recovery period

- Kageharu spends roughly 1.5 years recovering and honing himself.
- His Demon Slayer Mark/scar remains important and sometimes burns, especially in moonlight.

## Corps entry

- Kageharu meets Kagaya and the Hashira.
- He accepts a place in the Corps.
- He requests formal rank Kinoe until he earns anything higher through Corps service.
- His combat ability is recognized as Hashira-comparable.
- Moon Hashira rumors immediately begin and refuse to die.

## Butterfly Estate courtship

- Kageharu and Shinobu develop active mutual courtship.
- Flower-language gestures become a recurring system.
- They kiss and discuss future possibilities.
- Kageharu creates and hides a labradorite engagement ring at the Estate.
- Naho, Kiyo, and Sumi begin moving it daily as part of the ring-hunt game.

## Entertainment District assignment

- Tengen's wives go silent in Yoshiwara.
- Kagaya assigns Kageharu to accompany Tengen, Tanjiro, Zenitsu, and Inosuke.
- Reconnaissance reveals a human scout/bird network.
- Kageharu uses a female disguise to infiltrate socially.
- Captives are found beneath houses.
- Hinatsuru, Makio, and Suma are all rescued alive.
- Nezuko independently helps locate additional captives.

## Upper Rank Six battle

- Daki and Gyutaro are identified.
- Kageharu destabilizes Daki through insults and flirtation.
- Daki provides Doma/Eternal Paradise Faith and Kokushibo intelligence.
- Kageharu uses the secret Seventh Form.
- Severe physical backlash follows.
- Kageharu arranges simultaneous decapitation with silver/Nichirin wire.
- Daki and Gyutaro die.

## Night after battle

- Group returns to Tengen's house.
- Kageharu falls asleep immediately on a couch.
- He sleep-talks about Tengen needing flashier jewels.

## Morning after battle

- Kageharu wakes around 5 a.m. and cooks traditional breakfast.
- `Domestic Breathing` becomes a running joke/framework.
- He demonstrates massage/bodywork on Tengen's wives with permission.
- Lunch is packed.
- Kageharu gives Inosuke a curry pan containing a known fast-acting sleep medicine.
- Inosuke is packed into a basket for the road.

## Current scene

- Group is traveling toward Butterfly Estate.
- Kageharu carries sleeping Inosuke.
- Tanjiro carries Nezuko.
- Tengen and Zenitsu carry food/provisions.
- Kageharu says the drug should last about two hours.
- Shinobu already knows about the drug from a previous use.
- Destination: medical examination, reports, and reunion.
