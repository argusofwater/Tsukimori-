# Entertainment District AU

## Premise

This mission replaces the canon Entertainment District sequence in this AU.

Tengen's wives Suma, Makio, and Hinatsuru had gone silent while undercover in Yoshiwara.

Assignments:

- Makio: Ogimoto House
- Hinatsuru: Kyogoku House
- Suma: Tokito House

Kagaya assigned [[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu]] to accompany Tengen, Tanjiro, Zenitsu, and Inosuke.

Tengen held operational command and told Kageharu to speak up if he noticed something Tengen did not.

## Mission goals

- locate the wives
- investigate disappearances
- identify the demon threat
- protect civilians
- give Kageharu experience operating under a Hashira's command

## Reconnaissance phase

The group discovered a human scout and signaling network around the district.

Established details:

- road watchers monitored swordsmen, officials, and unusual travelers
- birds were used for signaling
- the group's description had circulated that morning
- disappearance patterns were normalized or ignored in certain houses
- records and memories around missing women were manipulated socially
- compartmentalized intermediaries prevented ordinary scouts from seeing the full system

Kageharu and Tengen staged a fake argument to disrupt surveillance. During it Kageharu called Tengen old, prompting the immortal line:

`I AM TWENTY-THREE!`

Kageharu later disabled scouts, stole tokens/documents/whistle/bird feed, and left them unconscious in trees after stripping them.

## Disguise infiltration

Kageharu disguised himself as an attractive woman with a moon fan and partially covered face.

During this sequence:

- rich men flirted with him and gave him roughly 20,000 yen in tips
- he complimented Tengen's jewels and powerful thighs while secretly passing him a report
- he flirted with Tanjiro while passing a note
- he flirted with Zenitsu and made him question his composure
- Zenitsu later complimented the disguise's backside without knowing it was Kageharu

This became a major post-mission joke.

## Hidden captives

Inosuke found impossible spaces and hidden women behind altered walls/floors.

Zenitsu identified internal knock-signals.

Tanjiro tracked fear and scent patterns.

Kageharu identified two demon presences:

- outside the district, extreme bloodlust later connected to Gyutaro
- an attractive woman hiding in plain sight, Daki

## Hinatsuru rescue

Kageharu and Tengen infiltrated a hidden basement and found Hinatsuru poisoned/sick but alive.

Hinatsuru had taken wisteria poison to make the demon woman leave her alone.

Kageharu had anticipated that possibility and carried an antidote from Shinobu. He gave it to Tengen for Hinatsuru.

Hinatsuru warned them about:

- obi
- women disappearing
- rooms changing
- people forgetting
- a network centered on something beyond a single ordinary woman

## Makio and Suma

Kageharu found Makio alive but bruised and battered. She nearly punched him after he announced himself with `Do not attack me im not your husband.`

Suma was later located by the boys after Zenitsu heard crying and Tanjiro tracked scent.

All three wives survived.

## Nezuko rescue work

Nezuko later left her box during the chaos and independently emerged supporting a little girl and holding a terrified young woman.

She indicated that more captives remained beneath houses.

Tengen, Tanjiro, Zenitsu, Inosuke, and Nezuko returned to extract them while Kageharu stayed behind with the wives and previously rescued women.

Exact total rescued civilians remains unstated.

## Daki disruption tactics

Kageharu deliberately destabilized Daki through alternating insults and flirtation.

Established beats include:

- insults about her hair, face, dress, and worth
- reversal into calling her cute/attractive
- removing face cloth and letting his hair down
- cutting obi and severing her right arm with a perfected Azure Arc sequence
- appearing behind her, catching her, kissing her cheek, dropping her, and walking away

Daki's reaction: `HE KISSED ME!`

The kiss was tactical and does not alter Kageharu's relationship with Shinobu.

## Doma and Kokushibo intelligence

Kageharu interrogated Daki at swordpoint.

He revealed his two major vendettas:

- Kokushibo killed Katsuhisa
- Doma killed Kageharu's family

Daki confirmed:

- Doma found dying Daki and Gyutaro and gave them blood
- Doma has followers/cultists in the Eternal Paradise Faith
- people come willingly to worship/confess
- women are especially relevant to his interests
- current exact location is unknown
- the network can be followed through cultists, recruiters, travel, and donations
- Doma appears beautiful, kind, smiling, joking, but is not
- `don't let him talk`
- Kokushibo is even more dangerous in a direct encounter

## Seventh Form

Kageharu requested distance and open space away from civilians.

He used:

`Hi no Kata: Mangetsu no Kanata....`

See [[Abilities/Veil Breathing]].

The technique reduced Daki and Gyutaro to regenerative mince-like states while intentionally sparing Daki's throat.

## Final kill

After the Seventh, Daki and Gyutaro regenerated.

Kageharu thanked Daki for the intelligence, expressed sorrow that her life had been stolen, and told her to rest.

He then prepared silver/Nichirin wire so that Gyutaro's movement and Daki's position produced simultaneous decapitation.

Both demons died and crumbled.

The final emotional beat remained quiet rather than celebratory.

## Aftermath

Kageharu suffered severe backlash from the Seventh:

- eye bleeding
- right-hand tremor
- gait disturbance
- exhaustion
- later right-arm twitching and self-reported possible muscular injury

Tengen agreed the Seventh Form's mechanics must be restricted from the general Corps report.
