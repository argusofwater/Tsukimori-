# Butterfly Estate Courtship

## Relationship status

[[Characters/Tsukimori Kageharu]] and [[Characters/Shinobu Kocho]] are actively courting.

They are not married and not formally engaged.

## Established affectionate language

Kageharu uses:

- `Shinobu-chan`
- `my butterfly`
- `favorite butterfly`

Shinobu has called him:

- `my moon`

That phrase reliably embarrasses him.

## Kisses

Established:

- forehead kisses
- cheek kisses
- lip kisses
- a deeper passionate kiss the morning before Kageharu left for the Entertainment District mission

## Flower-language courtship

Important flowers already used or discussed:

- Orange Ranunculus: radiant charm, fascination, attraction
- Orange Blossom: tied to Kageharu's one-month challenge and interpreted as a more serious declaration/intention toward lasting love and marriage
- Heliotrope: devotion / turning toward the sun / eyes drawn toward someone
- Jonquil / yellow daffodil: desire for returned affection without crushing pressure
- Apple Blossom: preference / good fortune / choosing someone
- Gorse: love in all seasons / patience
- Sweet William: gallantry / smile / finesse
- Mistletoe: surmount difficulties / give a kiss
- Myrtle: home, domestic happiness, steady affection

For current return from Yoshiwara, Kageharu plans **hyacinths** as apology flowers, tied to sorrow, apology, and forgiveness.

See [[Motifs/Flower Language and Courtship]].

## Future discussions

They have discussed a possible future containing:

- marriage
- a clinic
- three children as an early hypothetical
- names such as Chiyo, Runa, Asuki, Tsukimi, Hazuki, Mitsuki, Satsuki, Yuzuki
- Shinobu notably liking Chiyo, Hazuki, and Yuzuki, with Mitsuki considered strategically amusing
- a peaceful post-Muzan life
- Kageharu's mountain land and house
- possibly buying closer to the Estate later while keeping the mountain

These are future possibilities, not guarantees.

## Ring hunt

Kageharu created a labradorite engagement ring whose stone resembles a blue moon butterfly.

He hid it somewhere in the Butterfly Estate before leaving.

Naho, Kiyo, and Sumi are paid `triplet gremlins` who move it once daily. They may move it early if Shinobu gets close.

Finding the ring is **not** the proposal.

## Proposal plan

Kageharu intends to properly propose during a blue moon around three months after the current mission.

Planned structure:

- picnic
- long conversation
- seemingly lose track of time
- walk home through a broad clearing
- butterflies moving through the field
- blue moon overhead
- proposal in the clearing

The wives of Tengen heard this plan and strongly approved it.

## Pre-mission domestic intimacy

The night before departure, Kageharu carried Shinobu to her room, helped her change modestly into a silk gown, cuddled with her, scratched her back, and she slept deeply.

The next morning they shared a passionate goodbye kiss. Tengen opened the door afterward and teased them about Shinobu's hair.

## Current return tension

Kageharu has already sent Shinobu the full mission truth rather than trying to hide anything, including:

- Seventh Form consequences
- Kokushibo
- Doma
- Daki cheek kiss
- mission outcome

He expects the first day or two home to involve intense medical attention and limited freedom.

The tone should remain loving, sharp, witty, and medically grounded rather than turning Shinobu into a jealous caricature.
